SPECIFICATION ADDENDUM 001
National Federal Coverage, Comprehensive New Mexico Coverage, and Evidentiary Timestamping
Version: 1.0

Project repository: specs/

======================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY
======================================================================

This addendum modifies and supplements the Election Information Observatory
Complete Project Specification, Version 0.1 Draft.

This addendum makes three material changes:

1. It replaces the New-Mexico-only product boundary with national coverage of
   all federal races.
2. It preserves comprehensive coverage of all races and ballot questions
   published through the New Mexico election-results system.
3. It establishes a dedicated Digital Evidence and Trusted Timestamping
   subsystem intended to make acquisition times and preserved raw data as
   technically rigorous, independently verifiable, and legally defensible as
   practicable.

If this addendum conflicts with the original specification, this addendum
controls.

Nothing in this document guarantees admissibility in a particular court,
administrative proceeding, investigation, or other forum. Admissibility and
weight depend on applicable law, authentication, certifications, testimony,
chain of custody, and the circumstances of the proceeding. The system shall,
however, be designed to support authentication and independent verification.

======================================================================
2. REVISED PRODUCT SCOPE
======================================================================

2.1 Product Definition

The Election Information Observatory shall be:

- A national tracker for all federal election contests;
- A comprehensive tracker for all New Mexico election contests and ballot
  questions available from official sources;
- An immutable archive of raw official, aggregator, and reporting-source
  observations;
- A source-comparison and reporting-analysis platform; and
- A digital-evidence preservation system capable of exporting independently
  verifiable evidence packages.

2.2 National Federal Coverage

The system shall cover all applicable federal contests across the 50 states,
the District of Columbia, and relevant United States territories.

Required contest classes are:

A. PRESIDENT AND VICE PRESIDENT

- Statewide popular-vote returns;
- Electoral-vote allocation;
- Congressional-district allocation where applicable;
- Electoral College totals;
- Called, projected, unofficial, canvassed, recounted, certified, and amended
  states represented separately;
- Faithless-elector or post-election Electoral College events shall not be
  inferred from election-night returns and shall be handled as separate later
  events if included.

B. UNITED STATES SENATE

- Every regularly scheduled contest;
- Every special election;
- Each state's applicable seat/class designation;
- Senate-control tracking;
- Independents and caucusing relationships represented explicitly rather
  than automatically assigned to a party without documented support;
- Vice-presidential tie-breaking implications displayed separately from seat
  counts.

C. UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

- Every voting congressional district;
- Every special election;
- House-control tracking;
- At-large districts;
- Non-voting delegates and resident commissioner contests represented
  separately and excluded from the 218-seat voting-member majority threshold.

2.3 Comprehensive New Mexico Coverage

The system shall collect and track every contest and question published
through the applicable New Mexico official election-results sources,
including, when present:

- President and Vice President;
- United States Senate;
- United States House;
- Governor and Lieutenant Governor;
- Secretary of State;
- Attorney General;
- State Treasurer;
- State Auditor;
- Commissioner of Public Lands;
- Public Regulation Commission or successor/applicable commission contests;
- New Mexico Senate;
- New Mexico House of Representatives;
- Statewide judicial contests;
- Judicial retention questions;
- Public Education Commission;
- Metropolitan Court;
- Magistrate Court;
- County offices;
- Municipal offices included in the official election feed;
- College district contests or questions;
- Soil and Water Conservation District contests;
- Constitutional amendments;
- Statewide ballot questions;
- County and local questions;
- Special elections; and
- Any other contest, race, retention question, or ballot question carried in
  the official New Mexico result feed.

2.4 Geographic Resolution

For national federal contests, the system shall collect results at the finest
officially published and legally collectable resolution, including:

- Nation;
- State or jurisdiction;
- Congressional district;
- County or county equivalent;
- Precinct or reporting unit, when published; and
- Vote method, when published.

For New Mexico, the system shall additionally support all available state,
legislative, judicial, county, municipal, special-district, and ballot-question
geographies.

2.5 Revised Navigation

The principal tracker navigation shall include:

LIVE | PRESIDENT | SENATE | HOUSE | NEW MEXICO | MAP | SOURCES | ARCHIVE

3. REVISED SOURCE ACQUISITION SCOPE
======================================================================

3.1 Primary Official Sources

For every federal contest, the primary source shall be the responsible state,
District of Columbia, territorial, county, or local election authority that
publishes the result.

The system shall maintain a jurisdiction acquisition matrix covering:

- 50 states;
- District of Columbia;
- Relevant territories;
- County or local sources required to fill gaps in federal results;
- New Mexico comprehensive down-ballot sources; and
- Canvass, recount, certification, and amendment sources.

3.2 Secondary Sources

The system may collect independent observations from:

- Licensed national election-data aggregators;
- National reporting organizations;
- New Mexico local reporting organizations;
- Official announcements;
- Campaign or candidate statements;
- Publicly accessible correction and retraction notices; and
- Other approved research sources.

No aggregator or publisher shall replace the applicable official election
authority as the primary results source.

3.3 National Adapter Requirement

The source-adapter architecture shall support:

- Generic CSV;
- Generic JSON;
- Generic XML;
- Spreadsheet exports;
- Plain-text exports;
- HTML tables;
- JavaScript-rendered result applications;
- Common election-night reporting platforms;
- Custom state systems;
- County systems;
- PDF canvass and certification records; and
- Manual evidence intake under controlled procedures.

New Mexico shall serve as the full-depth reference jurisdiction, but the
canonical model shall be national before state-specific collector
implementation is frozen.

4. DIGITAL EVIDENCE AND TRUSTED TIMESTAMPING SUBSYSTEM
======================================================================

4.1 Objective

The subsystem shall preserve evidence sufficient for an independent examiner
to determine:

- The exact bytes acquired;
- The URL and source from which they were requested;
- The collection software and configuration used;
- The relevant request and response metadata;
- When the collector requested and received the data;
- The state and confidence of the collector clock;
- The cryptographic digest of the acquired artifact;
- That the artifact or an enclosing manifest existed no later than an
  independently attested time;
- The subsequent custody, processing, replication, and export history; and
- The relationship between the raw artifact and every parsed or normalized
  observation cited by the application.

4.2 Evidentiary Claim Boundary

The system may support the claim:

"These exact bytes were acquired and were in the archive no later than the
independently attested trusted timestamp."

The system shall not represent the timestamp alone as proving:

- That every visitor saw the same bytes;
- That the source created the bytes at that exact time;
- That the bytes were true or accurate;
- That an apparent discrepancy establishes intent or misconduct; or
- That the evidence is automatically admissible in every forum.

The following times shall remain distinct:

SOURCE-REPORTED TIME
!=
REQUEST TIME
!=
RESPONSE-RECEIPT TIME
!=
ARCHIVE-WRITE TIME
!=
HASH-CALCULATION TIME
!=
TRUSTED-TIMESTAMP TIME

4.3 Byte-Exact Preservation

For each acquisition, the system shall preserve the original response body
byte-for-byte before parsing, decoding, normalizing, reformatting, or
rendering.

A recommended acquisition directory is:

/acquisitions/{source_id}/{yyyy}/{mm}/{dd}/{acquisition_id}/
    response.bin
    request.json
    response_headers.json
    acquisition.json
    clock_status.json
    response.sha256
    timestamp_request.tsq
    timestamp_response.tsr
    collector_signature.sig

The response.bin object is the evidentiary original. Parsers shall operate
against an immutable reference or verified copy.

4.4 Cryptographic Digests

Each raw artifact shall receive at minimum:

- SHA-256 digest;
- Digest calculation timestamp;
- Digest implementation identification;
- Byte length; and
- Verification result after archive write.

The architecture shall permit future addition of a second digest algorithm
without changing or deleting the original digest record.

4.5 Required Timestamps

Each acquisition record shall independently preserve:

- scheduler_created_utc;
- request_created_utc;
- request_sent_utc;
- first_byte_received_utc;
- response_completed_utc;
- artifact_write_started_utc;
- artifact_write_completed_utc;
- hash_calculated_utc;
- manifest_inclusion_utc;
- trusted_timestamp_utc;
- parser_started_utc;
- parser_completed_utc;
- source_reported_timestamp, if present;
- source_reported_timezone, if known;
- source_reported_timestamp_raw; and
- first_observed_utc.

All application-generated timestamps shall use UTC and an unambiguous,
high-resolution format. The original source timestamp string shall be retained
without alteration.

4.6 Clock Integrity

Collector hosts shall synchronize against multiple independent, approved time
sources.

For each collection period, retain:

- Host identifier;
- Time synchronization service and version;
- Configured time sources;
- Active time source;
- NTP stratum or equivalent;
- Clock offset;
- Jitter or uncertainty;
- Synchronization state;
- Clock-step and clock-slew events;
- Last successful synchronization; and
- Any interval of degraded or unsynchronized time.

If time synchronization falls outside the approved tolerance, acquisitions
shall still be preserved but marked TIME_CONFIDENCE_DEGRADED.

The initial operational tolerance shall be defined during detailed design and
validated in rehearsal. A starting objective is collector-clock offset within
100 milliseconds during election-night operation, with alerting before the
offset reaches 500 milliseconds. These thresholds are engineering targets,
not substitutes for an independent trusted timestamp.

4.7 RFC 3161 Trusted Timestamps

The system shall obtain an independently verifiable trusted timestamp using an
RFC 3161 compliant Time Stamp Authority for either:

- Each raw artifact; or
- A cryptographic manifest or Merkle root that covers a defined batch of raw
  artifacts.

The timestamp request shall submit a digest, not the election content itself.

The system shall preserve:

- Timestamp request;
- Timestamp response/token;
- TSA policy identifier;
- TSA certificate;
- Full certificate chain;
- Signature algorithm;
- Message-imprint algorithm;
- Serial number;
- Generation time;
- Nonce, when used;
- Verification result; and
- Validation software and version.

Especially significant artifacts should be individually timestamped even if
routine acquisitions are batch timestamped. Significant events include:

- First published returns;
- Poll closure snapshots;
- Lead changes;
- Race calls;
- Corrections or retractions;
- Source outages or resumptions;
- Final election-night snapshots;
- Canvass results;
- Recount results;
- Certifications; and
- Amended certifications.

4.8 Tamper-Evident Chaining

Each acquisition ledger event shall reference the previous ledger event or
previous artifact-chain value.

At a defined interval, initially one to five minutes during active election
collection, the system shall:

1. Enumerate newly preserved artifacts;
2. Construct a deterministic manifest;
3. Calculate artifact hashes;
4. Construct a Merkle tree or equivalent tamper-evident aggregate;
5. Digitally sign the manifest/root;
6. Submit the root or manifest digest to the trusted timestamp authority;
7. Preserve the timestamp response;
8. Replicate the evidence batch; and
9. Verify the replicated copy.

4.9 Immutable Storage

Raw artifacts, timestamp tokens, signed manifests, and custody logs shall use:

- Object versioning;
- Write-once or object-lock retention controls;
- Separate production and evidence-administration roles;
- Least-privilege service accounts;
- Multi-factor authentication for evidence administrators;
- Replication to a second administrative boundary;
- Deletion protection;
- Retention-policy audit logs; and
- Periodic restore and integrity testing.

The public tracker and public API shall have no credential capable of changing
or deleting evidentiary originals.

4.10 Digital Signatures and Key Management

Collector manifests and evidence exports shall be digitally signed.

Signing keys shall be protected using a managed Hardware Security Module or
comparably controlled key-management service for production operation.

Required controls include:

- Key identifier;
- Key owner;
- Key creation date;
- Activation and retirement dates;
- Approved algorithms;
- Rotation procedure;
- Revocation procedure;
- Dual control for high-impact operations;
- Access audit logs;
- Backup and recovery procedure; and
- Preservation of certificates needed for later validation.

4.11 Chain of Custody

Every evidence object shall have an append-only custody ledger covering:

- Acquisition;
- Initial hash;
- Archive write;
- Post-write verification;
- Manifest inclusion;
- Trusted timestamping;
- Replication;
- Periodic integrity verification;
- Parser access;
- Analyst access where applicable;
- Export;
- Transfer;
- Legal hold;
- Retention extension; and
- Authorized disposition.

Each event shall contain:

- Event ID;
- Evidence ID;
- Event type;
- UTC time;
- Actor or service identity;
- Host or service identity;
- Reason;
- Prior state;
- New state;
- Related artifact hash;
- Authorization reference; and
- Digital signature or protected audit-log linkage.

4.12 Processing Lineage

Every parsed or normalized observation shall identify:

- Original artifact ID;
- Original artifact hash;
- Parser name and version;
- Parser source-code revision;
- Configuration revision;
- Parsing start and completion times;
- Validation findings;
- Canonical mapping version;
- Human review events; and
- Derived calculation version.

A parser correction shall generate a new derived observation. It shall not
alter the original artifact or erase the previous parsed result.

4.13 Evidence Quality Levels

E0 - UNVERIFIED
Artifact exists, but integrity or timestamp evidence is incomplete.

E1 - HASHED
Exact artifact has a verified cryptographic digest and acquisition metadata.

E2 - CHAINED
Artifact is included in a signed, append-only manifest or Merkle structure.

E3 - INDEPENDENTLY TIMESTAMPED
Artifact or its covering manifest has a successfully verified independent
RFC 3161 trusted timestamp.

E4 - REPLICATED AND REVERIFIED
E3 evidence has been replicated across an administrative boundary and the
replica has been cryptographically reverified with custody events recorded.

Production election evidence shall target E4. The tracker shall not cite an
artifact below E2 without visibly identifying the degraded evidence state.

======================================================================

5.1 Export Structure

The system shall export a self-contained evidence package for any cited
observation:

EIO-EVIDENCE-{election}-{jurisdiction}-{evidence_id}/

    ORIGINAL/
        response.bin

    ACQUISITION/
        request.json
        response_headers.json
        acquisition.json
        clock_status.json

    INTEGRITY/
        response.sha256
        manifest.json
        manifest.sig
        merkle_proof.json
        timestamp_request.tsq
        timestamp_response.tsr
        certificate_chain/

    PROCESSING/
        parser_version.json
        parser_source_revision.txt
        parsed_observation.json
        normalization.json
        validation_findings.json

    CUSTODY/
        chain_of_custody.json
        replication_events.json
        verification_events.json

    EXPORT/
        export_manifest.json
        export_manifest.sig

    README.txt
    VERIFY.txt

5.2 Independent Verification

The evidence package shall permit an examiner without production-database
access to:

- Recalculate the original artifact digest;
- Verify the signed manifest;
- Verify the Merkle inclusion proof, if used;
- Verify the RFC 3161 timestamp token;
- Validate the available certificate chain and preserved validation material;
- Confirm the artifact-to-parser relationship;
- Reproduce the parsed observation using the preserved parser revision where
  practical; and
- Review custody and replication history.

5.3 Verification Utility

The project shall provide an offline verification utility that:

- Accepts an evidence-package path;
- Performs structural checks;
- Recalculates hashes;
- Verifies signatures;
- Verifies timestamp tokens;
- Verifies Merkle inclusion;
- Reports missing or expired validation material;
- Produces a machine-readable report; and
- Produces a human-readable verification report.

The utility shall not require access to the production environment.

6. AUTHENTICATION AND RECORDS PRACTICE
======================================================================

6.1 Design Objective

The system and supporting operating procedures shall be designed to support
authentication of electronic records through:

- Evidence describing the collection process;
- Evidence showing that the process produces accurate results;
- Cryptographic identification of the copied data;
- Certifications by qualified custodians or technical personnel when needed;
- Repeatable verification procedures; and
- Documented chain of custody.

6.2 Written Operating Procedures

The project shall maintain approved procedures for:

- Collector deployment;
- Time synchronization;
- Source configuration;
- Collection failure handling;
- Raw evidence preservation;
- Hashing;
- Trusted timestamping;
- Manifest signing;
- Key management;
- Replication;
- Verification;
- Analyst access;
- Evidence export;
- Legal hold;
- Incident response;
- Certificate and validation-material preservation; and
- Record disposition.

6.3 Custodian and Technical Declarations

The system shall be capable of generating factual reports supporting a
custodian or qualified technical declaration, including:

- System purpose;
- Process description;
- Software and version;
- Source configuration;
- Relevant logs;
- Hashes;
- Timestamp verification;
- Chain-of-custody events;
- Known errors or outages; and
- Reproduction instructions.

The system shall not automatically assert legal conclusions.

7. LONG-TERM VALIDATION
======================================================================

7.1 Preservation Material

For each trusted timestamp and digital signature, preserve:

- End-entity certificate;
- Intermediate certificates;
- Root certificate reference or copy where permissible;
- Timestamp token;
- TSA policy identifier;
- Certificate status material available at validation time;
- Signature and digest algorithm identifiers;
- Validation report;
- Validation software and version; and
- Relevant trust-policy version.

7.2 Renewal and Migration

The evidence program shall include periodic review of:

- Cryptographic algorithm strength;
- Certificate expiration;
- TSA availability;
- Trust-anchor changes;
- Storage-medium health;
- File-format obsolescence; and
- Verification-tool compatibility.

Before an algorithm or certificate chain becomes unsuitable for long-term
validation, evidence sets shall be re-attested or wrapped in a new signed and
trusted-timestamped preservation manifest without altering the originals.

8. SECURITY AND FAILURE REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

8.1 Failure Handling

If the TSA is unavailable:

- Continue preserving raw artifacts;
- Hash each artifact immediately;
- Include artifacts in locally signed manifests;
- Queue timestamp requests durably;
- Record the outage;
- Submit the earliest possible trusted timestamp when service resumes;
- Mark evidence quality below E3 until successful; and
- Do not backdate the trusted timestamp.

If the collector clock is degraded:

- Preserve the acquisition;
- Flag clock confidence;
- Continue using the independent timestamp service;
- Alert operators; and
- Preserve synchronization diagnostics.

If immutable storage is unavailable:

- Preserve data in protected local spool storage;
- Calculate hashes immediately;
- Sign spool manifests;
- Timestamp the manifest when possible;
- Alert operators;
- Transfer to immutable storage once restored; and
- Record the complete custody sequence.

8.2 Separation of Duties

No single routine operator shall be able to:

- Modify raw evidence;
- Delete raw evidence;
- Change retention policy;
- Replace a signing key;
- Alter custody logs; and
- approve the same high-impact action.

9. NEW AND MODIFIED REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-SCOPE-001
The platform shall track all federal election contests nationally.

REQ-SCOPE-002
The platform shall track every New Mexico contest and ballot question
available from approved official results sources.

REQ-SCOPE-003
The canonical election model shall support national federal coverage before
state collector implementation is frozen.

REQ-EVID-001 - EVIDENTIARY ACQUISITION
Every raw election-data artifact used by the Election Information Observatory
shall be preserved byte-for-byte before parsing, assigned a collision-
resistant cryptographic digest, incorporated into a tamper-evident append-only
evidence chain, stored using immutable retention controls, replicated across
administrative boundaries, and covered by an independently verifiable trusted
timestamp conforming to RFC 3161. The system shall independently preserve
source-reported time, acquisition time, system clock state, trusted timestamp
time, processing history, and chain-of-custody events.

REQ-EVID-002 - REPRODUCIBLE EVIDENCE PACKAGE
The system shall export any cited observation as a self-contained evidence
package allowing an independent examiner to verify the original bytes,
cryptographic digest, acquisition metadata, trusted timestamp, certificate
chain, processing lineage, and chain of custody without access to the
production database.

REQ-EVID-003 - EXACT-BYTE ORIGINAL
The evidentiary original shall be the exact response body acquired from the
source, preserved before transformation.

REQ-EVID-004 - DISTINCT TIMES
The system shall preserve source-reported, request, response, archive, hash,
manifest, and trusted timestamp times as separate facts.

REQ-EVID-005 - TRUSTED THIRD-PARTY TIME
Production evidence shall be covered by an independently verifiable RFC 3161
trusted timestamp.

REQ-EVID-006 - CLOCK MONITORING
Collector hosts shall continuously measure and preserve clock-synchronization
state and uncertainty.

REQ-EVID-007 - EVIDENCE CHAIN
Artifacts shall be incorporated into a signed tamper-evident chain or Merkle
manifest.

REQ-EVID-008 - IMMUTABLE STORAGE
Evidence shall be protected by versioning, retention controls, deletion
protection, and separated administrative roles.

REQ-EVID-009 - CROSS-BOUNDARY REPLICATION
Production evidence shall be replicated to a second administrative boundary
and cryptographically reverified.

REQ-EVID-010 - APPEND-ONLY CUSTODY
All evidence handling shall be recorded in an append-only custody ledger.

REQ-EVID-011 - PROCESSING LINEAGE
Every displayed or analyzed observation shall trace to an artifact, parser
revision, configuration revision, and canonical mapping revision.

REQ-EVID-012 - OFFLINE VERIFICATION
Evidence packages shall be verifiable without production-system access.

REQ-EVID-013 - TSA FAILURE MODE
The system shall continue collection during TSA outages without falsely
representing delayed timestamps as contemporaneous timestamps.

REQ-EVID-014 - LONG-TERM VALIDATION
The project shall preserve certificates, timestamp tokens, validation
material, algorithms, policies, and verification reports required for later
validation.

REQ-EVID-015 - CLAIM LIMITATION
The application shall accurately describe what the timestamp and archive do
and do not prove.

13. CONCLUSION
======================================================================

The Election Information Observatory is nationally scoped for all federal
races and comprehensively scoped for all New Mexico races and ballot
questions. Its raw-data archive is not an ordinary application log. It is a
dedicated evidence system designed to preserve exact source bytes, provenance,
clock state, cryptographic identity, independent proof of existence,
processing lineage, and chain of custody.

The system's evidentiary design shall support a precise and limited claim:
that identified bytes were acquired, preserved, and independently attested as
existing no later than a verifiable time. All broader claims about what a
source generated, what other users observed, the truth of the content, or the
legal effect of the evidence require additional facts and appropriate legal
analysis.
