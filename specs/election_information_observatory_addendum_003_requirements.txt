ELECTION INFORMATION OBSERVATORY
SPECIFICATION ADDENDUM 003
Requirements for Observation Completeness, Capture Consistency, Source Authority, Time Semantics, and Evidence-System Qualification
Version: 1.0 Draft
Date: 2026-11-03
Project repository: specs/

======================================================================
1. PURPOSE, STATUS, AND PRECEDENCE
======================================================================

1.1 Purpose

This addendum closes remaining requirements gaps identified during review of
Addendum 002. It establishes additional and revised requirements for:

- Failed acquisition evidence;
- Negative-observation semantics;
- Source authority and official-publication designation;
- Timestamp precision, accuracy, authority, and confidence;
- Acquisition consistency and atomicity;
- Transport bytes versus decoded content bytes;
- Source-result version identity;
- Official-source hierarchy and conflict presentation;
- Certified public-record acquisition;
- National time-zone and poll-close modeling;
- Ranked-choice and multi-round tabulation;
- Observed numeric delta terminology;
- Schema versioning and canonical serialization;
- Deterministic Merkle-tree construction;
- Signed software build provenance;
- Expected-source and expected-contest completeness accounting;
- Pre-election baseline evidence;
- Explicit election-results presentation semantics;
- Deterministic evidence-state predicates;
- Evidence System Qualification Records; and
- External witness and rehearsal records.

1.2 Status

This document is a requirements addendum. It supplements the master
specification, Addendum 001, and Addendum 002.

1.3 Precedence

Where this addendum conflicts with Addendum 002, this addendum controls. Where
this addendum is silent, Addendum 002, Addendum 001, and the master
specification remain in force in that order.

1.4 Legal Qualification

These requirements are intended to support authentication, integrity,
reproducibility, and defensible records practices. They do not guarantee
admissibility or establish the truth of acquired content. Final procedures,
certification language, legal-hold policy, retention schedules, and evidence
production practices require review by qualified legal counsel.

2. FAILED ACQUISITIONS AND NEGATIVE OBSERVATIONS
======================================================================

REQ-OBS-001 - EVERY ATTEMPT IS AN ACQUISITION EVENT

Every scheduled or manually initiated acquisition attempt shall create an
append-only acquisition event regardless of whether content is successfully
returned.

The event shall include:

- acquisition_id;
- source_id;
- active source-configuration version;
- scheduler-created time;
- scheduled collection time;
- attempt-start time;
- attempt-completion time;
- collector host and release identity;
- requested URL;
- attempt number;
- retry relationship;
- collection stage reached;
- DNS result;
- connection result;
- TLS result;
- HTTP result, if any;
- response-byte count, if any;
- failure classification;
- exception or error identifier;
- configured timeout;
- actual elapsed time;
- clock-confidence status;
- next scheduled retry;
- operator intervention, if any; and
- protected audit-log reference.

REQ-OBS-002 - FAILURE TAXONOMY

Failed and incomplete acquisitions shall use explicit statuses, including:

- SCHEDULER_FAILED;
- DNS_FAILED;
- CONNECTION_FAILED;
- TLS_FAILED;
- CERTIFICATE_VALIDATION_FAILED;
- REDIRECT_POLICY_FAILED;
- AUTHENTICATION_FAILED;
- AUTHORIZATION_DENIED;
- RATE_LIMITED;
- HTTP_CLIENT_ERROR;
- HTTP_SERVER_ERROR;
- TIMEOUT_BEFORE_HEADERS;
- TIMEOUT_DURING_BODY;
- CONNECTION_RESET;
- PARTIAL_RESPONSE;
- EMPTY_RESPONSE;
- INVALID_CONTENT_TYPE;
- DECOMPRESSION_FAILED;
- BROWSER_FAILED;
- PAGE_LOAD_FAILED;
- DEPENDENT_RESOURCE_FAILED;
- PARSE_FAILED;
- ARCHIVE_WRITE_FAILED;
- HASH_FAILED;
- TIMESTAMP_FAILED;
- VERIFICATION_FAILED;
- OPERATOR_ABORTED; and
- UNKNOWN_FAILURE.

REQ-OBS-003 - NEGATIVE-OBSERVATION SEMANTICS

The system shall distinguish at minimum:

- OBSERVED_CHANGED;
- OBSERVED_UNCHANGED;
- OBSERVATION_FAILED;
- OBSERVATION_INDETERMINATE;
- SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE;
- SOURCE_RETURNED_EMPTY;
- SOURCE_RETURNED_INVALID;
- SOURCE_RETURNED_PARTIAL;
- SOURCE_VERSION_UNCHANGED;
- SOURCE_VERSION_CHANGED;
- NOT_SCHEDULED; and
- COLLECTION_SUPPRESSED_BY_POLICY.

The timeline shall not represent failed or indeterminate intervals as though
the source was observed and unchanged.

REQ-OBS-004 - FAILURE EVIDENCE PRESERVATION

Failure metadata, diagnostic records, relevant network evidence, and related
logs shall receive the same append-only, cryptographic, timestamp, retention,
and replication protections applicable to successful acquisition metadata.

REQ-OBS-005 - RETRY TRANSPARENCY

Retries shall create distinct acquisition events linked to the originating
scheduled collection. The system shall not collapse several failed attempts
and a later successful attempt into a single apparent successful observation.

3. SOURCE AUTHORITY AND PUBLICATION DESIGNATION
======================================================================

REQ-SOURCE-001 - SOURCE AUTHORITY RECORD

Every source classified as official shall have a versioned Source Authority
Record containing:

- source_id;
- owning election authority;
- jurisdiction;
- authority level;
- election scope;
- officially designated publication page;
- official endpoint or vendor endpoint;
- results-system provider;
- hosting provider, when known;
- CDN provider, when known;
- authority-to-vendor relationship;
- official designation artifact;
- verification method;
- verifier;
- independent reviewer;
- verification date;
- activation date;
- expiration or revalidation date;
- supporting URLs or documents;
- supporting artifact digests;
- approval status; and
- known limitations.

REQ-SOURCE-002 - AUTHORITY CHAIN

For vendor-hosted or externally hosted official systems, the system shall
preserve the publication chain, for example:

Election Authority
    -> Official Election Page
    -> Designated Vendor Platform
    -> Hosting/CDN Endpoint
    -> Collector

Network endpoint authentication shall not by itself establish that an endpoint
is officially designated by the election authority.

REQ-SOURCE-003 - AUTHORITY REVALIDATION

P0 official sources shall be revalidated:

- Before each covered election;
- After an endpoint or registrable-domain change;
- After an unexpected certificate or ownership change;
- After a vendor-platform migration;
- After an official authority announcement affecting publication; and
- Following a material source compromise or incident.

REQ-SOURCE-004 - SOURCE CLASSIFICATION

Each source shall be explicitly classified as one or more of:

- PRIMARY_OFFICIAL;
- SUBJURISDICTION_OFFICIAL;
- OFFICIAL_CERTIFICATION_RECORD;
- AUTHORIZED_AGGREGATOR;
- INDEPENDENT_AGGREGATOR;
- NATIONAL_REPORTING_SOURCE;
- LOCAL_REPORTING_SOURCE;
- CAMPAIGN_OR_PARTY_SOURCE;
- PUBLIC_STATEMENT_SOURCE;
- RESEARCH_SOURCE; and
- UNKNOWN_OR_UNVERIFIED.

4. TIME SEMANTICS
======================================================================

REQ-TIME-001 - STRUCTURED TIME VALUE

Every material timestamp shall be represented by a structured object including:

- timestamp value;
- UTC offset;
- IANA time-zone identifier, when applicable;
- original source string;
- time authority;
- technical precision;
- estimated accuracy or uncertainty;
- synchronization state;
- confidence state;
- leap-second handling policy;
- time-zone database version, when converted; and
- derivation method, if not directly observed.

REQ-TIME-002 - PRECISION IS NOT ACCURACY

The system shall represent time precision and time accuracy separately. A
microsecond-formatted timestamp shall not be described as accurate to one
microsecond solely because of its formatting.

REQ-TIME-003 - TIME AUTHORITY VALUES

Time authority shall distinguish at minimum:

- SOURCE_DISPLAYED;
- SOURCE_MACHINE_READABLE;
- COLLECTOR_SYSTEM_CLOCK;
- BROWSER_SYSTEM_CLOCK;
- HTTP_DATE_HEADER;
- RFC3161_TSA;
- EXTERNAL_PUBLICATION_ANCHOR;
- OPERATOR_ENTERED;
- DERIVED;
- UNKNOWN.

REQ-TIME-004 - TIME CONFIDENCE VALUES

Time confidence shall distinguish at minimum:

- independently_attested;
- synchronized_within_policy;
- synchronized_outside_target;
- degraded;
- unsynchronized;
- source_supplied_unverified;
- derived;
- ambiguous;
- unknown.

REQ-TIME-005 - POLL-CLOSE TIME MODEL

Poll-close records shall include:

- jurisdiction;
- statutory or officially announced close time;
- IANA time zone;
- UTC conversion;
- source and source artifact;
- extension orders or announcements;
- affected precincts or jurisdictions;
- effective time;
- confidence state; and
- supersession relationship.

The tracker shall not infer that polls are closed solely because a configured
clock time has passed when official status or legally authorized extensions
indicate otherwise.

REQ-TIME-006 - MULTIPLE LOCAL TIMES

The national model shall support elections or jurisdictions spanning multiple
time zones or multiple poll-close times. State-level display shall not conceal
material differences within the state.

5. ACQUISITION CONSISTENCY AND ATOMICITY
======================================================================

REQ-CONSIST-001 - CAPTURE INTERVAL

Every acquisition bundle shall record:

- capture_started_utc;
- capture_completed_utc;
- capture_duration_ms;
- each component's start and completion time;
- each component's source-reported generation or update time;
- each component's source version identifier; and
- ordering relationships among components.

REQ-CONSIST-002 - CONSISTENCY CLASSIFICATION

Multi-component acquisitions shall be classified as:

- CONSISTENT;
- LIKELY_CONSISTENT;
- POSSIBLY_MIXED;
- KNOWN_MIXED;
- UNDETERMINED;
- NOT_APPLICABLE.

REQ-CONSIST-003 - CONSISTENCY RULES

The consistency determination shall use documented rules based on available
source-version identifiers, ETags, update timestamps, request ordering,
network events, DOM state, and source behavior.

REQ-CONSIST-004 - NO FALSE ATOMICITY

The tracker and analysis engine shall not represent a multi-resource bundle as
an atomic source state when the evidence indicates that components may have
come from different source versions.

REQ-CONSIST-005 - RECAPTURE ON MIXED STATE

When a bundle is classified POSSIBLY_MIXED or KNOWN_MIXED, the collector shall
attempt a bounded recapture under the configured policy. The original mixed
bundle shall remain preserved.

REQ-CONSIST-006 - DATA/SCREENSHOT ASSOCIATION

A screenshot or DOM snapshot shall identify the exact API/network responses
known to have contributed to the rendered view where technically feasible. If
that relationship cannot be established, the system shall mark it UNKNOWN.

6. TRANSPORT BYTES AND DECODED CONTENT
======================================================================

REQ-BYTE-001 - BYTE-LAYER DEFINITIONS

The evidence specification shall distinguish:

- WIRE_OR_TRANSPORT_PAYLOAD: bytes as received at the capture layer before
  content decoding, when technically available;
- HTTP_ENTITY_PAYLOAD: bytes constituting the HTTP response entity;
- DECODED_CONTENT_PAYLOAD: bytes after Content-Encoding decompression;
- TEXT_DECODED_REPRESENTATION: characters after charset decoding; and
- NORMALIZED_APPLICATION_REPRESENTATION: parsed or transformed data.

REQ-BYTE-002 - ORIGINAL EVIDENTIARY PAYLOAD

For each collector type, the specification shall explicitly define which byte
layer is the evidentiary original and whether earlier transport-layer bytes
are available. The collector shall not claim preservation of wire bytes when
its HTTP or browser library only exposes decoded content.

REQ-BYTE-003 - MULTI-LAYER DIGESTS

When both transport and decoded forms are available, the system shall preserve
and independently hash each form. Their relationship shall be recorded in the
bundle manifest.

REQ-BYTE-004 - CONTENT DECODING EVIDENCE

Preserve:

- Content-Encoding;
- Transfer-Encoding;
- charset declaration;
- decompression implementation and version;
- decoding implementation and version;
- input and output byte lengths;
- decoding errors; and
- digest before and after transformation.

REQ-BYTE-005 - NO UNDECLARED NORMALIZATION

Line-ending conversion, Unicode normalization, character replacement,
whitespace changes, JSON reserialization, spreadsheet recalculation, or other
transformation shall never be applied to the evidentiary original.

7. SOURCE RESULT VERSION IDENTITY
======================================================================

REQ-VERSION-001 - SOURCE VERSION FIELDS

The system shall preserve, where available:

- source_version_id;
- source_sequence_number;
- source_generation_time;
- source_update_time;
- export_generation_time;
- ETag;
- Last-Modified;
- source file name;
- source report identifier;
- source batch identifier;
- application build/version displayed by the source; and
- source-specific revision metadata.

REQ-VERSION-002 - ACQUISITION VERSUS SOURCE VERSION

Multiple acquisition events may reference the same source result version. The
system shall distinguish continued observation of an unchanged version from a
new source publication.

REQ-VERSION-003 - VERSION COMPARISON

The change engine shall compare both artifact identity and source-version
identity. Identical source version identifiers with changed bytes, or changed
version identifiers with identical bytes, shall generate reviewable anomaly
events.

REQ-VERSION-004 - VERSION SUPERSESSION

Where a source identifies replacement or supersession, the system shall
preserve the relationship without deleting the superseded version.

======================================================================
8. OFFICIAL SOURCE HIERARCHY AND CONFLICTS
======================================================================

REQ-AUTH-001 - SOURCE PRIORITY POLICY

Each contest or jurisdiction shall have a versioned source-priority policy
identifying:

- primary display source;
- primary official source;
- subjurisdiction official sources;
- fallback source;
- authorized aggregator role;
- staleness thresholds;
- failover conditions;
- restoration conditions; and
- conflict-presentation rules.

REQ-AUTH-002 - FIELD-LEVEL AUTHORITY

Authority may differ by field. The system shall permit one source to be
primary for vote totals and another official source to be primary for ballot
status, outstanding-ballot estimates, certification documents, or precinct
metadata.

REQ-AUTH-003 - OFFICIAL CONFLICT STATE

When relevant official sources materially disagree and the difference is not
explained by update time, scope, ballot type, or source version, the tracker
shall display an OFFICIAL_SOURCES_DIFFER status rather than silently selecting
one value.

REQ-AUTH-004 - FALLBACK DISCLOSURE

When the displayed value comes from a fallback or non-primary source, the UI
and API shall identify the source, the reason for fallback, and the time the
primary source became unavailable or stale.

REQ-AUTH-005 - NO UNATTRIBUTED CONSENSUS

The system shall not create an unattributed consensus value by averaging,
majority vote, or silent selection among disagreeing official or reporting
sources.

9. CERTIFICATION AND OFFICIAL PUBLIC RECORDS
======================================================================

REQ-CERTREC-001 - CERTIFICATION RECORD TYPE

Certified election records shall have a dedicated record type distinct from
election-night result snapshots.

REQ-CERTREC-002 - CERTIFICATION METADATA

Preserve, where available:

- issuing body;
- jurisdiction;
- election;
- contest scope;
- certification date;
- document title;
- document identifier;
- certified-copy status;
- signer identity and official capacity as represented in the record;
- seal or attestation information;
- filing or publication location;
- original digital signature;
- signature certificate and chain;
- signature validation result;
- timestamp embedded in the official document;
- supersedes relationship;
- superseded-by relationship; and
- amendment or correction status.

REQ-CERTREC-003 - PRESERVE ISSUER SIGNATURE

When an authority provides a digitally signed document, preserve the original
signed object and signature validation evidence. The Observatory's own trusted
timestamp supplements and does not replace the issuer's signature.

REQ-CERTREC-004 - CERTIFIED COPY STATUS

The system shall distinguish:

- official publication;
- certified copy;
- public-facing informational copy;
- scanned copy;
- reporter or third-party copy;
- machine-readable official export; and
- unknown status.

REQ-CERTREC-005 - CERTIFICATION RECONCILIATION

Certified and amended certified totals shall be reconciled to prior unofficial
and canvassed observations without overwriting any stage.

10. RANKED-CHOICE AND MULTI-ROUND TABULATION
======================================================================

REQ-RCV-001 - TABULATION ENTITY

Ranked-choice or multi-round contests shall use a tabulation entity containing:

- tabulation_id;
- contest_id;
- source_id;
- source version;
- tabulation method;
- tabulation start/completion times;
- number of rounds;
- final-round indicator;
- certification status; and
- governing rule reference.

REQ-RCV-002 - ROUND ENTITY

Each round shall preserve:

- round_number;
- continuing candidates;
- eliminated candidate or candidates;
- candidate totals;
- transferred votes;
- exhausted ballots;
- inactive ballots;
- undervotes;
- overvotes;
- round total;
- threshold, if applicable;
- source labels; and
- source artifact.

REQ-RCV-003 - TRANSFERS ARE NOT NEW BALLOTS

Vote transfers between ranked-choice rounds shall not be labeled as newly
reported ballots or new votes.

REQ-RCV-004 - MULTIPLE TABULATIONS

The system shall support preliminary, corrected, recounted, and certified
ranked-choice tabulations without merging their rounds.

REQ-RCV-005 - METHOD EXTENSIBILITY

The model shall permit instant-runoff voting, single transferable vote where
relevant, sequential elimination, and source-defined multi-round methods
without assuming one universal algorithm.

11. CHANGE AND DELTA TERMINOLOGY
======================================================================

REQ-DELTA-001 - OBSERVED NUMERIC DELTA

The default computed difference between observations shall be labeled
OBSERVED_NUMERIC_DELTA, not NEW_VOTES.

REQ-DELTA-002 - DELTA CAUSE CLASSIFICATION

A numeric delta may be classified as:

- NEW_BALLOTS_REPORTED;
- NORMAL_COUNT_UPDATE;
- CORRECTION;
- REALLOCATION;
- RANKED_CHOICE_TRANSFER;
- REPORTING_UNIT_ADDITION;
- REPORTING_UNIT_REMOVAL;
- BALLOT_METHOD_ADDITION;
- BALLOT_METHOD_RECLASSIFICATION;
- RECOUNT_CHANGE;
- CERTIFICATION_CHANGE;
- SOURCE_SCOPE_CHANGE;
- ROUNDING_CHANGE; or
- UNKNOWN_CAUSE.

REQ-DELTA-003 - NEW VOTES LABEL RESTRICTION

The UI and API may use the label NEW_VOTES only when source semantics or
validated reconciliation support that interpretation.

REQ-DELTA-004 - NEGATIVE DELTAS

Negative deltas shall remain visible and shall not be silently clamped to zero.
They shall be classified or marked UNKNOWN_CAUSE.

12. SCHEMA VERSIONING AND CANONICAL SERIALIZATION
======================================================================

REQ-SCHEMA-001 - SCHEMA IDENTITY

Every structured record shall identify:

- schema_name;
- schema_version;
- schema_digest;
- schema_uri or repository reference;
- serialization format;
- canonicalization method;
- producing software release; and
- migration version, if applicable.

REQ-SCHEMA-002 - PRESERVED HISTORICAL SCHEMAS

Every schema version used to create evidence or analytical records shall be
preserved for the applicable retention period.

REQ-SCHEMA-003 - NO SILENT REINTERPRETATION

Historical records shall not be silently interpreted under a later schema.
Migration shall create a new versioned representation linked to the original.

REQ-CANON-001 - CANONICAL SERIALIZATION

Signed manifests, Merkle leaves, custody events, source configurations, and
other cryptographically protected structured records shall use a specified
canonical serialization, such as deterministic CBOR or an approved canonical
JSON scheme.

REQ-CANON-002 - CANONICALIZATION PROFILE

The canonicalization profile shall define:

- character encoding;
- key ordering;
- number representation;
- date/time representation;
- Unicode handling;
- whitespace treatment;
- null handling;
- duplicate-key handling;
- binary-data encoding; and
- unsupported-value behavior.

REQ-CANON-003 - CROSS-IMPLEMENTATION TEST

At least two independent implementations shall produce identical canonical
bytes and digests for the shared conformance test corpus.

13. DETERMINISTIC MERKLE-TREE REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-MERKLE-001 - VERSIONED TREE PROFILE

Every Merkle tree shall identify a versioned tree construction profile.

REQ-MERKLE-002 - COMPLETE TREE DEFINITION

The profile shall define:

- leaf input format;
- leaf domain-separation prefix;
- internal-node domain-separation prefix;
- digest algorithm;
- canonical artifact ordering;
- duplicate-leaf handling;
- concatenation order;
- binary encoding;
- odd-leaf handling;
- empty-tree behavior;
- tree balancing method;
- proof format;
- root serialization; and
- renewal behavior.

REQ-MERKLE-003 - DETERMINISTIC ROOT

Given the same ordered set of leaf inputs and profile version, independent
implementations shall produce the same root.

REQ-MERKLE-004 - ERRATA AND STANDARDS TRACKING

When an external evidence-record or Merkle-related standard is used, the
project shall document applicable errata, implementation choices, and
conformance tests.

REQ-MERKLE-005 - PROOF PRESERVATION

The system shall preserve sufficient inclusion-proof material to verify an
artifact independently without requiring production-database access.

14. SOFTWARE BUILD PROVENANCE
======================================================================

REQ-BUILD-001 - SIGNED BUILD PROVENANCE

Each production release shall have a signed provenance statement containing:

- source repository identity;
- source commit;
- commit-signature status;
- build runner identity;
- build workflow identity;
- build recipe;
- build time;
- build input digests;
- dependency digests;
- base image digest;
- output artifact digest;
- SBOM digest;
- builder attestation; and
- signing identity.

REQ-BUILD-002 - BUILD/DEPLOY RELATIONSHIP

Deployment records shall identify the exact signed build provenance and
artifact digest deployed to each collector and parser host.

REQ-BUILD-003 - SUPPLY-CHAIN VERIFICATION

Only releases passing signature, provenance, vulnerability-policy, SBOM, and
artifact-digest checks may be deployed to production collectors, except under
an audited break-glass process.

REQ-BUILD-004 - REPRODUCIBLE OR VERIFIABLE BUILD

The project shall document whether builds are reproducible bit-for-bit. If not,
the alternative verification and provenance controls shall be explicitly
stated.

15. EXPECTED COVERAGE AND COMPLETENESS
======================================================================

REQ-COMP-001 - ELECTION COVERAGE MANIFEST

Each covered election shall have a signed, versioned Election Coverage
Manifest containing:

- expected federal contests;
- expected New Mexico contests and questions;
- expected jurisdictions;
- expected official sources;
- expected fallback sources;
- expected certification sources;
- expected collector configuration;
- expected start/end collection windows;
- responsible operator; and
- known accepted exclusions.

REQ-COMP-002 - COMPLETENESS STATES

For contests and sources, track:

- EXPECTED;
- CONFIGURED;
- VERIFIED;
- ACTIVE;
- HEALTHY;
- DEGRADED;
- FAILED;
- NOT_STARTED;
- COMPLETED;
- EXCLUDED_WITH_APPROVAL; and
- UNEXPECTED_DISCOVERY.

REQ-COMP-003 - REAL-TIME COVERAGE DASHBOARD

Election-night operations shall display counts and lists of expected,
configured, verified, active, healthy, degraded, failed, and missing sources
and contests.

REQ-COMP-004 - SILENT-GAP PROHIBITION

A source or contest expected by the Election Coverage Manifest but absent from
collection shall generate an alert and remain visible as a coverage gap.

REQ-COMP-005 - COVERAGE CHANGE CONTROL

Adding, removing, or excluding a P0 contest or source during an active election
shall require an auditable reason, approver, activation time, and revised
signed coverage manifest.

16. PRE-ELECTION BASELINE EVIDENCE
======================================================================

REQ-BASE-001 - BASELINE COLLECTION

Before polls close, the system shall acquire and preserve baseline evidence
for:

- Contest definitions;
- Candidate and ballot-choice lists;
- Candidate/source crosswalks;
- District and reporting-unit definitions;
- Poll-close schedules;
- Official result URLs;
- Source Authority Records;
- Source certificates and endpoint observations;
- Expected downloadable exports;
- Result-page display structure;
- Election-status labels;
- Ballot-method definitions;
- Reporting-status definitions; and
- Zero-result or pre-results files where published.

REQ-BASE-002 - BASELINE DEADLINES

P0 baseline collection shall be completed and reviewed at least seven days
before the election, with a final revalidation no later than 24 hours before
the first covered poll closes, unless the authority publishes required data
later.

REQ-BASE-003 - BASELINE MANIFEST

Baseline artifacts shall be included in a signed, independently timestamped
baseline manifest linked to the Election Coverage Manifest.

REQ-BASE-004 - BASELINE CHANGE DETECTION

Changes occurring after baseline approval shall generate difference records
and alerts for review.

17. ELECTION-RESULT PRESENTATION SEMANTICS
======================================================================

REQ-PRES-001 - SOURCE-SPECIFIC REPORTING MEASURES

The system shall preserve distinct measures including:

- precincts fully reporting;
- precincts partially reporting;
- total precincts;
- voting locations reporting;
- counties reporting;
- estimated vote reporting;
- ballots reported;
- ballots remaining;
- estimated ballots remaining;
- registered voters;
- eligible voters;
- turnout;
- ballot types included;
- ballot types outstanding;
- next expected update; and
- source-defined reporting measures.

REQ-PRES-002 - NO UNIVERSAL PERCENT REPORTING

The system shall not collapse materially different source measures into one
unqualified percent_reporting field.

REQ-PRES-003 - PRECICTS ARE NOT BALLOTS

The system shall never infer that the percentage of precincts reporting equals
the percentage of ballots counted.

REQ-PRES-004 - ESTIMATE LABELING

Estimated vote reporting, estimated outstanding ballots, and modeled turnout
shall be labeled ESTIMATED and attributed to the estimating source.

REQ-PRES-005 - BALLOT-INCLUSION CONTEXT

Where published, the tracker shall present which ballot classes are included
in the current totals and whether provisional, mail, overseas, write-in, early,
or Election Day ballots remain outstanding.

18. DETERMINISTIC EVIDENCE-STATE PREDICATES
======================================================================

REQ-STATE-001 - EXPLICIT PREDICATES

The system shall calculate evidence state from explicit Boolean or enumerated
predicates, including:

- SOURCE_AUTHORITY_VERIFIED;
- ENDPOINT_VERIFIED;
- TRANSACTION_CAPTURED;
- ARTIFACT_HASH_VERIFIED;
- BUNDLE_SIGNATURE_VERIFIED;
- TRUSTED_TIMESTAMP_VERIFIED;
- SECOND_ANCHOR_VERIFIED;
- IMMUTABLE_STORAGE_VERIFIED;
- REPLICA_VERIFIED;
- CONFIGURATION_VERIFIED;
- COLLECTOR_RELEASE_VERIFIED;
- PARSER_RELEASE_VERIFIED;
- CROSSWALK_VERIFIED;
- CONSISTENCY_VERIFIED;
- CLOCK_WITHIN_POLICY; and
- CUSTODY_CHAIN_VERIFIED.

REQ-STATE-002 - NO OPAQUE TRUST SCORE

The platform shall not replace explicit evidence conditions with an opaque
numeric trust or confidence score.

REQ-STATE-003 - MACHINE-READABLE STATE

Evidence predicates and their supporting references shall be available through
the API and evidence package.

REQ-STATE-004 - UI DISCLOSURE

The tracker shall summarize degraded evidence states without implying that
content is false merely because a collection or verification control is
incomplete.

19. EVIDENCE SYSTEM QUALIFICATION RECORD
======================================================================

REQ-QUAL-001 - QUALIFICATION RECORD

Each production election shall have an Evidence System Qualification Record
covering the exact production architecture and release set used for that
election.

REQ-QUAL-002 - QUALIFICATION CONTENT

The record shall include:

- system architecture version;
- collector and parser releases;
- source configuration set;
- Election Coverage Manifest;
- baseline manifest;
- clock synchronization tests;
- hash implementation tests;
- canonicalization tests;
- Merkle conformance tests;
- TSA verification tests;
- dual-anchor tests;
- signing-key health and access review;
- immutable-storage tests;
- replication and restore tests;
- parser replay tests;
- browser capture tests;
- failure-mode tests;
- evidence-package verification tests;
- security assessment status;
- unresolved known defects;
- approved risk acceptances;
- operator training records;
- procedure versions;
- incident-response contacts; and
- final authorization to operate for the election.

REQ-QUAL-003 - QUALIFICATION FREEZE

A qualification record shall be signed and independently timestamped before
the production election window. Material changes afterward shall create an
amendment and documented impact assessment.

REQ-QUAL-004 - KNOWN DEFECT TRANSPARENCY

Known defects affecting collection, timing, integrity, interpretation, or
coverage shall be recorded. A production authorization shall identify accepted
risks and compensating controls.

REQ-QUAL-005 - EVIDENCE ABOUT THE EVIDENCE SYSTEM

Qualification records, test results, procedure versions, training records,
and election-specific authorization records shall receive evidence-grade
retention and integrity protection.

======================================================================
20. INDEPENDENT WITNESS AND REHEARSAL RECORDS
======================================================================

REQ-WIT-001 - NATIONAL REHEARSAL

Before public production use, the system shall conduct an end-to-end national
rehearsal using historical or synthetic replay data clearly labeled for test
use.

REQ-WIT-002 - INDEPENDENT OBSERVER

At least one qualified person not responsible for implementing the tested
component shall witness or independently review critical qualification tests,
including evidence export and offline verification.

REQ-WIT-003 - REHEARSAL RECORD

The rehearsal record shall preserve:

- scope;
- participants;
- systems and releases;
- test data identity;
- start/end times;
- test procedures;
- results;
- failures;
- corrective actions;
- retest evidence;
- observer notes; and
- approval decision.

REQ-WIT-004 - EXTERNAL VERIFICATION EXERCISE

An evidence package shall be provided to an independent examiner operating
without production access. The examiner shall verify hashes, signatures,
trusted timestamps, Merkle proofs, schema/canonicalization, processing lineage,
and available custody records.

REQ-WIT-005 - NO SYNTHETIC/PRODUCTION CONFUSION

Synthetic rehearsal artifacts shall use separate source IDs, election IDs,
storage prefixes, signing context, and display labeling so they cannot be
mistaken for production election evidence.

21. ADDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE TESTS
======================================================================

AT-003-001 - FAILED ACQUISITION
Simulate DNS, TLS, timeout, HTTP 503, partial-body, and archive-write failures.
Verify each attempt creates a distinct protected acquisition event.

AT-003-002 - NEGATIVE OBSERVATION
Verify the timeline distinguishes observed unchanged, failed observation,
indeterminate observation, and source unavailable.

AT-003-003 - SOURCE AUTHORITY CHAIN
Verify a vendor-hosted official results site can be traced from election
authority designation through vendor and endpoint evidence.

AT-003-004 - TIME PRECISION/ACCURACY
Verify microsecond-formatted timestamps retain a separate measured or estimated
accuracy value and time authority.

AT-003-005 - POLL-CLOSE EXTENSION
Apply an official poll-close extension to selected precincts and verify the
tracker does not mark those precincts closed based only on the original time.

AT-003-006 - MIXED DYNAMIC CAPTURE
Cause an API result update between page dependencies and screenshot capture.
Verify the bundle is marked POSSIBLY_MIXED or KNOWN_MIXED and is not presented
as atomic.

AT-003-007 - TRANSPORT/DECODED BYTES
Acquire gzip-encoded content and verify separate transport and decoded digests
when both layers are available.

AT-003-008 - UNDECLARED NORMALIZATION
Alter line endings or JSON formatting in a derived copy and verify the original
artifact remains unchanged and independently identifiable.

AT-003-009 - SOURCE VERSION IDENTITY
Acquire the same source version repeatedly, then acquire a new version. Verify
continued observation and source publication change are distinguished.

AT-003-010 - OFFICIAL SOURCE CONFLICT
Create conflicting state and county official totals not explained by time or
scope. Verify OFFICIAL_SOURCES_DIFFER is displayed.

AT-003-011 - FALLBACK DISCLOSURE
Disable the primary source and verify fallback values are visibly attributed
with reason and activation time.

AT-003-012 - SIGNED CERTIFICATION DOCUMENT
Acquire a digitally signed certification document and verify preservation and
validation of the issuer signature in addition to Observatory timestamps.

AT-003-013 - RANKED-CHOICE TABULATION
Replay a multi-round contest and verify transfers and exhausted ballots are
not counted as newly reported ballots.

AT-003-014 - OBSERVED NUMERIC DELTA
Verify unexplained candidate-total changes are labeled observed numeric deltas,
not new votes.

AT-003-015 - CANONICAL SERIALIZATION
Verify two independent implementations produce identical canonical bytes and
digests for the conformance corpus.

AT-003-016 - MERKLE DETERMINISM
Verify two independent implementations produce identical roots and inclusion
proofs for the same ordered leaves and tree profile.

AT-003-017 - HISTORICAL SCHEMA
Open an old evidence package using its preserved schema and verify no silent
reinterpretation under the current schema.

AT-003-018 - BUILD PROVENANCE
Verify source commit, build runner, inputs, recipe, output digest, SBOM, and
signed provenance for a deployed collector.

AT-003-019 - COVERAGE GAP
Remove one expected House contest and verify the completeness dashboard and
alerts identify the missing contest.

AT-003-020 - BASELINE CHANGE
Change a source endpoint or candidate list after baseline approval and verify a
reviewable difference event.

AT-003-021 - REPORTING MEASURE
Verify precinct reporting, estimated vote reporting, and ballots remaining are
stored and displayed as distinct measures.

AT-003-022 - EVIDENCE PREDICATES
Invalidate one trust anchor and verify explicit predicate changes without an
opaque trust score.

AT-003-023 - SYSTEM QUALIFICATION
Generate and verify a signed election-specific Evidence System Qualification
Record containing test results, known defects, risk acceptances, and approval.

AT-003-024 - INDEPENDENT EXAMINER
Provide a package to an isolated verification environment and obtain a signed
verification report without production access.

24. REVISED FREEZE CRITERIA
======================================================================

The architecture may proceed to detailed design freeze only when:

- All Addendum 001, 002, and 003 P0 requirements have assigned owners;
- Every P0 requirement maps to an acceptance test;
- Failed acquisitions are treated as first-class evidence;
- Source authority is distinguished from endpoint identity;
- Time precision, accuracy, authority, and confidence are separately modeled;
- Multi-component consistency is represented without false atomicity;
- The evidentiary byte layer is defined for every collector type;
- Canonical serialization is selected and tested;
- Merkle-tree construction is fully deterministic and versioned;
- Schemas and migrations are evidence-bearing and preserved;
- Expected-source and expected-contest completeness are measurable;
- Baseline evidence and source-authority review are defined;
- The Evidence System Qualification Record is approved;
- National election semantics cover multiple rounds and official-source
  conflicts;
- The external verification procedure is defined; and
- No unresolved gap permits missing collection intervals to appear as observed
  unchanged intervals.

25. CONCLUSION
======================================================================

Addendum 003 extends the Election Information Observatory from a strong
forensic acquisition architecture into a complete observation-accountability
system. It requires the platform to preserve not only successful content, but
also failed attempts, indeterminate intervals, source authority, endpoint
identity, byte-layer meaning, capture consistency, software qualification,
and expected coverage.

The system shall be capable of demonstrating:

- What it intended to collect;
- Why a source was considered authoritative;
- What endpoint it contacted;
- What happened during every attempt;
- Which exact bytes were preserved;
- Which transformations occurred;
- Whether a multi-resource capture represented a coherent source state;
- Which source version was observed;
- What the system could not observe;
- Whether official sources disagreed;
- Whether the collection system was qualified for production; and
- Whether an independent examiner can reproduce the cryptographic and
  processing verification without production access.

This narrows the defensible factual claim appropriately: the Observatory can
show what was attempted, what was acquired, how it was preserved, what was
not acquired, how the source was identified, and what limitations applied at
the time. It shall not convert evidentiary completeness into a claim that the
source content was substantively correct or that any discrepancy establishes
intent or misconduct.
