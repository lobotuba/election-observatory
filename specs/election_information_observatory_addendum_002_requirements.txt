======================================================================
1. PURPOSE, STATUS, AND PRECEDENCE
======================================================================

1.1 Purpose

This addendum closes material gaps identified during review of Addendum 001.
It establishes revised and additional requirements for:

- Source endpoint authentication;
- Complete HTTP and browser acquisition bundles;
- Dynamic-page and dependent-resource capture;
- Cryptographic protection of evidentiary logs;
- Source-configuration governance;
- Reproducible collection and parsing environments;
- Long-term evidence records and cryptographic renewal;
- Algorithm agility;
- Content-addressed evidence storage;
- Correction and revision classification;
- Missing-data semantics;
- National federal-election edge cases;
- Candidate and contest identity;
- Legal holds;
- Separation of duties and resistance to privileged-administrator compromise;
- Independent external anchoring; and
- Evidence-package access profiles.

1.2 Status

This document is a requirements addendum. It does not replace the system's
complete master specification. It modifies and supplements Addendum 001.

1.3 Precedence

Where this addendum conflicts with Addendum 001, this addendum controls.
Where neither addendum addresses a subject, the master specification remains
in force.

1.4 Legal Qualification

The requirements are designed to support authentication, integrity,
reproducibility, and defensible records practices. They do not guarantee
admissibility or establish the truth of acquired content. Legal counsel shall
review final evidence-retention, certification, legal-hold, disclosure, and
production procedures before production use.

2. REVISED EVIDENTIARY OBJECT MODEL
======================================================================

2.1 Fundamental Evidentiary Object

The fundamental evidentiary object shall be the ACQUISITION EVENT, not merely
the downloaded response body.

An acquisition event shall bind together:

- Source identity evidence;
- Network endpoint evidence;
- Request evidence;
- Response evidence;
- Content evidence;
- Browser-render evidence, when applicable;
- Clock-state evidence;
- Cryptographic digests;
- Manifest and Merkle inclusion evidence;
- Trusted timestamps;
- Custody events;
- Processing lineage; and
- Verification results.

2.2 Acquisition Bundle

Each acquisition event shall produce an acquisition bundle with a unique
acquisition_id. A bundle may contain:

ACQUISITION/{acquisition_id}/
    SOURCE_IDENTITY/
        source_registry_record.json
        source_configuration.json
        dns_observation.json
        tls_session.json
        server_certificate.der
        server_certificate_chain/

    REQUEST/
        request_metadata.json
        request_headers_public.json
        request_headers_restricted.enc
        request_body.bin

    RESPONSE/
        response_metadata.json
        response_headers.json
        response.bin
        redirect_chain.json

    BROWSER/
        browser_metadata.json
        network_log.json
        dom_snapshot.html
        screenshot.png
        console_log.json
        dependent_resources_manifest.json

    TIME/
        clock_status.json
        source_reported_time.json

    INTEGRITY/
        digests.json
        bundle_manifest.json
        bundle_manifest.sig
        merkle_proof.json
        timestamp_request.tsq
        timestamp_response.tsr
        evidence_record.ers

    PROCESSING/
        collection_release.json
        parser_release.json
        parsed_observation.json
        normalization.json
        validation_findings.json

    CUSTODY/
        custody_events.json
        replication_events.json
        verification_events.json

Not every source requires every component. The manifest shall explicitly mark
each component as PRESENT, NOT_APPLICABLE, NOT_COLLECTED, COLLECTION_FAILED,
RESTRICTED, or WITHHELD.

2.3 Artifact and Acquisition Distinction

The system shall distinguish:

- artifact_id: Identity of unique byte content;
- acquisition_id: Identity of an observation event;
- bundle_id: Identity of the complete evidentiary acquisition bundle; and
- evidence_record_id: Identity of the long-term validation record.

Identical bytes acquired 100 times may map to one content-addressed artifact
and 100 acquisition events. Each acquisition event remains independently
preserved because it establishes observation at a distinct time.

3. SOURCE ENDPOINT AUTHENTICATION REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-EVID-016 - SOURCE ENDPOINT AUTHENTICATION

For every network acquisition, the system shall preserve evidence sufficient
to identify the endpoint contacted and the security context of the connection.

Required fields include:

- Original requested URL;
- Final resolved URL;
- Complete redirect chain;
- Request hostname;
- DNS query time;
- DNS resolver identity;
- DNS answers used;
- Remote IP address;
- Remote port;
- Collector host identity;
- Collector source IP where observable;
- Proxy, VPN, gateway, or NAT path identifier where applicable;
- Server Name Indication hostname;
- TLS protocol version;
- TLS cipher suite;
- Server leaf certificate;
- Server certificate chain;
- Certificate fingerprints;
- Subject Alternative Names;
- Certificate validity interval;
- Certificate validation result;
- Certificate revocation/status result where available; and
- HTTP protocol version.

Acceptance criteria:

- An examiner can determine which hostname and network endpoint were contacted.
- An examiner can verify the preserved certificate fingerprints.
- Redirects cannot be silently omitted.
- Endpoint validation failures remain in the record and trigger an alert.

REQ-EVID-018 - DNS AND CERTIFICATE ANOMALY HANDLING

The system shall alert and preserve a separate event when:

- DNS answers materially change;
- A certificate changes;
- Certificate validation fails;
- A hostname redirects to a new registrable domain;
- An endpoint unexpectedly changes hosting provider or autonomous system;
- Plain HTTP replaces HTTPS; or
- TLS security falls below approved policy.

No anomalous acquisition shall be discarded solely because of the anomaly.
It shall be preserved and marked for review.

4. HTTP TRANSACTION AND SENSITIVE METADATA REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-EVID-019 - COMPLETE TRANSACTION METADATA

The acquisition bundle shall preserve:

- HTTP method;
- Full URL;
- Query parameters;
- Request headers;
- Request body where present;
- Response status;
- Response headers;
- Response body;
- Redirects;
- Retry history;
- Connection timing;
- Content encoding;
- Transfer encoding;
- Decompression status;
- Byte length before and after decompression; and
- Cache-related headers.

REQ-EVID-020 - SENSITIVE-HEADER SEGREGATION

Authentication headers, session tokens, cookies, API keys, internal hostnames,
and similar sensitive material shall not be placed in public evidence
packages.

The system shall:

- Record that a sensitive field existed;
- Preserve a cryptographic commitment to the original restricted record;
- Encrypt the restricted metadata;
- Apply a separate access-control policy;
- Log every access; and
- Support production of a redacted public representation and a restricted
  legal/security representation.

Secret values shall not be written into routine application logs.

REQ-EVID-021 - CDN AND CACHE EVIDENCE

Where present, preserve headers and observations relevant to caching,
including:

- Date;
- Age;
- ETag;
- Last-Modified;
- Cache-Control;
- Expires;
- Via;
- Vary;
- Warning;
- X-Cache or provider-equivalent headers;
- CDN request identifiers; and
- Edge-location identifiers.

The comparison engine shall use these fields when evaluating apparent source
latency or differing versions.

5. DYNAMIC PAGE AND BROWSER ACQUISITION REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-EVID-022 - DYNAMIC ACQUISITION BUNDLE

For web pages whose displayed election data depends on JavaScript, XHR, fetch,
GraphQL, WebSocket, Server-Sent Events, or other dependent requests, the system
shall preserve a browser acquisition bundle.

The bundle shall include, where technically and legally feasible:

- Initial HTML response;
- Election-data API responses;
- Relevant XHR/fetch responses;
- WebSocket or Server-Sent Event messages used to update displayed results;
- DOM snapshot after defined stabilization criteria;
- Full-page screenshot;
- Browser network log;
- Browser console log;
- Browser name and version;
- Browser binary digest;
- Operating-system or container image digest;
- Viewport;
- Device scale factor;
- User agent;
- Locale;
- Browser time zone;
- JavaScript enabled state;
- Capture start and completion time; and
- Dependent-resource manifest.

REQ-EVID-023 - TRANSPORT, RENDER, AND APPLICATION SEPARATION

The system shall treat the following as distinct evidence layers:

1. TRANSPORT EVIDENCE
   Raw HTTP/API traffic and responses.

2. RENDER EVIDENCE
   DOM snapshots and screenshots showing what the controlled browser rendered.

3. APPLICATION EVIDENCE
   Parsed, normalized, and derived election observations.

A screenshot shall not substitute for raw machine-readable data when the raw
data is available.

REQ-EVID-024 - BROWSER STABILIZATION RULE

The browser collector shall use a documented stabilization rule, such as:

- Required selector present;
- Relevant network requests completed;
- Network idle interval reached;
- Data timestamp located;
- Maximum capture timeout reached; and
- Final screenshot and DOM captured together.

The exact rule and result shall be included in browser_metadata.json.

REQ-EVID-025 - ACQUISITION-BUNDLE INTEGRITY

Each component in a bundle shall have its own digest. The bundle manifest shall
list all components, component status, digest algorithm, digest value, byte
length, collection time, and sensitivity profile. The signed bundle manifest,
not a screenshot alone, is the integrity root for the acquisition.

6. EVIDENTIARY AUDIT LOGGING REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-EVID-017 - EVIDENTIARY AUDIT LOGGING

Logs supporting authenticity and chain of custody shall receive evidence-grade
protection comparable to election artifacts.

Protected logs shall include:

- Collector activity;
- Collection errors;
- Authentication events;
- Authorization events;
- Administrative actions;
- Clock synchronization;
- Storage operations;
- Retention-policy changes;
- TSA requests and responses;
- HSM and key-management actions;
- Source-configuration changes;
- Deployment actions;
- Parser executions;
- Manual reviews;
- Evidence access;
- Evidence exports;
- Legal-hold actions; and
- Verification results.

REQ-EVID-026 - AUDIT LOG INTEGRITY

Audit logs shall be:

- Append-only;
- Time synchronized;
- Digitally signed or cryptographically chained;
- Replicated to a separate security boundary;
- Protected from alteration by routine administrators;
- Included in periodic trusted-timestamped manifests;
- Retained according to evidence-retention policy; and
- Searchable by evidence ID, acquisition ID, actor, action, and time.

REQ-EVID-027 - AUDIT LOG COMPLETENESS MONITORING

The system shall alert on:

- Missing log intervals;
- Sequence gaps;
- Invalid signatures;
- Unexpected clock discontinuities;
- Logging-service failure;
- Audit-store replication failure; and
- Administrative action without a corresponding audit event.

7. SOURCE CONFIGURATION GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-CONFIG-001 - VERSIONED SOURCE CONFIGURATION

Every source configuration shall be version controlled and contain:

- Source ID;
- Organization and jurisdiction;
- Official-source classification;
- Approved endpoint URLs;
- Collection method;
- Adapter version;
- Polling policy;
- Authentication method;
- Expected formats;
- Expected certificates or domains where appropriate;
- Terms and licensing status;
- Activation and expiration dates;
- Owner;
- Reviewer;
- Approval record; and
- Configuration digest.

REQ-CONFIG-002 - CONFIGURATION CHANGE CONTROL

Every production configuration change shall record:

- Change ID;
- Requestor;
- Author;
- Independent reviewer;
- Approval;
- Reason;
- Previous version;
- New version;
- Test evidence;
- Activation time;
- Rollback method;
- Git commit or equivalent revision; and
- Digital signature or signed release manifest.

REQ-CONFIG-003 - SOURCE VERIFICATION REVIEW

Before a P0 official source becomes active for an election, a documented source
verification review shall confirm:

- The source is controlled or designated by the applicable authority;
- The endpoint is correct;
- The expected contests and jurisdictions are present;
- Update behavior is understood;
- Timestamp behavior is understood;
- Download formats are tested;
- Alternate/fallback sources are recorded;
- Terms and rate limits are reviewed; and
- A second person approves activation.

REQ-CONFIG-004 - CONFIGURATION EVIDENCE

The active source configuration and its approval record shall be included by
reference and digest in each acquisition bundle.

8. REPRODUCIBLE SOFTWARE ENVIRONMENT REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-REPRO-001 - SIGNED RELEASE IDENTITY

Every production collector, parser, normalizer, comparison service, and
verification utility shall execute from an identifiable signed release.

Preserve:

- Source-control commit;
- Signed tag;
- Build identifier;
- OCI container image digest or equivalent package digest;
- Container image or durable artifact;
- Software Bill of Materials;
- Dependency lockfiles;
- Runtime version;
- Base image digest;
- Build workflow identity;
- Build provenance;
- Configuration schema version; and
- Database migration version.

REQ-REPRO-002 - SBOM FORMAT

The project shall generate a machine-readable SBOM in SPDX, CycloneDX, or both.
The selected format and version shall be documented.

REQ-REPRO-003 - REPRODUCIBLE PARSING

For every parsed observation, the system shall identify the exact parser
release, container digest, configuration digest, canonical mapping version,
and input artifact digest.

REQ-REPRO-004 - PRESERVED EXECUTION ENVIRONMENT

Production release images and required dependencies shall be retained for the
duration of the corresponding evidence-retention period or preserved through
a documented emulation/migration strategy.

REQ-REPRO-005 - RELEASE VERIFICATION

Before election-night use, release signatures, image digests, SBOM generation,
and parser replay shall pass automated verification.

9. CRYPTOGRAPHIC AGILITY AND LONG-TERM EVIDENCE RECORDS
======================================================================

REQ-CRYPTO-001 - ALGORITHM-AGILE DIGEST MODEL

Digests shall be stored as a collection, not as a single fixed field.

Required structure:

- Algorithm identifier;
- Algorithm object identifier where applicable;
- Digest value;
- Calculation time;
- Implementation and version;
- Input byte length; and
- Verification status.

SHA-256 shall be the minimum initial digest algorithm. The design shall permit
SHA-384, SHA-512, SHA3-256, SHA3-384, SHA3-512, or successor algorithms without
schema replacement.

REQ-CRYPTO-002 - ALGORITHM POLICY

A versioned cryptographic policy shall define approved:

- Digest algorithms;
- Signature algorithms;
- Key types and sizes;
- Timestamp algorithms;
- Certificate profiles;
- TLS versions;
- Renewal thresholds; and
- Deprecation procedures.

REQ-CRYPTO-003 - EVIDENCE RECORD STANDARD

The long-term archive design shall evaluate and document use of an Evidence
Record Syntax compatible with RFC 4998 or an equivalent standards-based
long-term evidence-record mechanism.

The selected mechanism shall support:

- Archive timestamps;
- Timestamp chains or sequences;
- Renewal without altering original artifacts;
- Merkle-tree or hash-tree inclusion;
- Verification after certificate expiration; and
- Migration before cryptographic algorithms become unsuitable.

REQ-CRYPTO-004 - PERIODIC EVIDENCE RENEWAL

The system shall periodically evaluate evidence records and renew protection
before:

- A digest algorithm becomes unsuitable;
- A signing algorithm becomes unsuitable;
- A TSA certificate expires without sufficient preserved validation material;
- A trust anchor is retired;
- A storage format becomes unsupported; or
- Verification software becomes unavailable.

Renewal shall wrap or re-attest existing evidence. It shall not alter the
original bytes or erase prior timestamp chains.

REQ-CRYPTO-005 - DUAL TRUST ANCHOR

Election-night root manifests shall be anchored through at least two
independent mechanisms selected from:

- Primary RFC 3161 TSA;
- Secondary independent RFC 3161 TSA;
- Public transparency log;
- Independent archival partner; or
- Another approved external proof-of-publication service.

At least one anchor shall be outside the operator's administrative control.

REQ-CRYPTO-006 - ANCHOR FAILURE HANDLING

Failure of one trust anchor shall not stop acquisition. The system shall:

- Preserve and hash artifacts;
- Sign local manifests;
- Submit to available anchors;
- Queue failed anchor requests;
- Record the outage;
- Preserve the actual later attestation time; and
- Avoid representing delayed anchoring as contemporaneous.

10. CONTENT-ADDRESSED STORAGE REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-STORE-001 - CONTENT ADDRESSING

Unique byte content shall receive an artifact ID derived from an approved
cryptographic digest or shall be indexed by that digest.

REQ-STORE-002 - ACQUISITION RETENTION

Deduplication of content shall not deduplicate or erase acquisition events.
Every observation time, endpoint, request, clock state, and custody event shall
remain separately recorded.

REQ-STORE-003 - REFERENCE INTEGRITY

Every acquisition referencing a shared artifact shall include:

- Artifact ID;
- Artifact digest;
- Acquisition ID;
- Bundle ID;
- Verification result; and
- Immutable storage location reference.

REQ-STORE-004 - SAFE GARBAGE COLLECTION

No artifact may be deleted while referenced by:

- An acquisition event;
- Evidence record;
- Legal hold;
- Exported evidence package;
- Certification package; or
- Retention policy.

11. DATA SEMANTICS AND REVISION REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-DATA-001 - MISSING DATA STATES

The system shall distinguish at minimum:

- ZERO;
- NULL_UNKNOWN;
- NOT_PUBLISHED;
- NOT_APPLICABLE;
- NOT_YET_OBSERVED;
- SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE;
- PARSER_FAILED;
- WITHHELD;
- REDACTED;
- CONFLICTING; and
- UNKNOWN_REASON.

A missing value shall never be silently converted to zero.

REQ-DATA-002 - CORRECTION AND CHANGE TAXONOMY

Every material change shall be classified, where evidence permits, as one of:

- NORMAL_COUNT_UPDATE;
- SOURCE_CORRECTION;
- SOURCE_RETRACTION;
- SOURCE_REPUBLICATION;
- PARSER_CORRECTION;
- NORMALIZATION_CORRECTION;
- CANONICAL_MAPPING_CORRECTION;
- APPLICATION_DISPLAY_CORRECTION;
- CANVASS_ADJUSTMENT;
- RECOUNT_ADJUSTMENT;
- CERTIFICATION_ADJUSTMENT;
- AMENDED_CERTIFICATION;
- LATE_BALLOT_INCLUSION;
- BALLOT_METHOD_RECLASSIFICATION;
- UNKNOWN_REVISION.

REQ-DATA-003 - REVISION PROVENANCE

A correction or revision shall record:

- Prior observation;
- New observation;
- Classification;
- Detecting system or reviewer;
- Supporting artifact(s);
- Time first observed;
- Time classified;
- Confidence;
- Review status; and
- Explanatory note.

REQ-DATA-004 - NO SILENT REPLACEMENT

Corrections to parser logic, canonical mappings, or displays shall create new
versioned outputs. Prior outputs shall remain preserved and traceable.

12. NATIONAL FEDERAL ELECTION SEMANTICS
======================================================================

REQ-ELECT-001 - ELECTION STAGE

The canonical model shall support:

- PRIMARY;
- PRIMARY_RUNOFF;
- CAUCUS;
- CONVENTION;
- GENERAL;
- GENERAL_RUNOFF;
- SPECIAL;
- SPECIAL_RUNOFF;
- RANKED_CHOICE_ROUND;
- RECOUNT;
- CANVASS;
- CERTIFICATION;
- AMENDED_CERTIFICATION; and
- OTHER_SOURCE_DEFINED_STAGE.

REQ-ELECT-002 - PRESIDENTIAL ALLOCATION

The model shall support:

- Statewide winner-take-all allocation;
- Maine congressional-district allocation;
- Nebraska congressional-district allocation;
- Statewide and district electoral-vote units;
- Elector slates;
- Write-in presidential candidates;
- Fusion or cross-endorsed ballot lines when present; and
- Separation of projected electoral votes from formally appointed electors.

REQ-ELECT-003 - SENATE CONTESTS

The model shall support:

- Regular and special Senate contests occurring in the same jurisdiction and
  cycle;
- Seat class;
- Vacancy status;
- Runoffs;
- Top-two or jungle-primary systems;
- Independents;
- Documented caucusing alignment; and
- Separation of seat counts from vice-presidential tie-breaking analysis.

REQ-ELECT-004 - HOUSE CONTESTS

The model shall support:

- Numbered districts;
- At-large districts;
- Special elections;
- Runoffs;
- Ranked-choice counting rounds;
- Non-voting delegates;
- Resident commissioner contests; and
- Exclusion of non-voting seats from the 218-seat majority calculation.

REQ-ELECT-005 - CONTEST STATUS

Contest status shall distinguish:

- NOT_STARTED;
- POLLS_OPEN;
- POLLS_CLOSED;
- REPORTING;
- LEADER_ONLY;
- PROJECTED_BY_SOURCE;
- UNOFFICIAL_COMPLETE;
- RUNOFF_REQUIRED;
- RECOUNT_PENDING;
- RECOUNT_IN_PROGRESS;
- CANVASSED;
- CERTIFIED;
- AMENDED;
- VACANT;
- CANCELED; and
- UNKNOWN.

REQ-ELECT-006 - SOURCE-SPECIFIC CALLS

A race call shall always be attributed to a source. The application shall not
collapse differing source calls into a single unattributed truth value.

13. CANDIDATE, PARTY, AND CONTEST IDENTITY REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-ID-001 - CANONICAL CANDIDATE IDENTITY

Candidates shall not be keyed solely by name. A canonical candidate record
shall support:

- Canonical candidate ID;
- Ballot name;
- Full name where available;
- Source-specific IDs;
- Contest-specific candidacy ID;
- Party or affiliation;
- Ballot line;
- Write-in status;
- Incumbency as source-reported metadata;
- Name variants; and
- Crosswalk provenance.

REQ-ID-002 - CANDIDACY VERSUS PERSON

The system shall distinguish a person from a candidacy. The same person may
have different candidacies across offices, elections, parties, or ballot
lines.

REQ-ID-003 - PARTY AND FUSION SUPPORT

The model shall support:

- Major parties;
- Minor parties;
- Independent or unaffiliated candidates;
- Multiple ballot lines;
- Fusion candidacies;
- Source-specific party labels; and
- Party-label changes without rewriting historical records.

REQ-ID-004 - CONTEST IDENTITY

Canonical contest identity shall include:

- Election;
- Stage;
- Office;
- Jurisdiction;
- District;
- Seat/class where applicable;
- Term type;
- Special-election indicator; and
- Vote-for-N rule.

REQ-ID-005 - CROSSWALK REVIEW

Ambiguous candidate or contest mappings shall be marked for human review and
shall not be silently merged.

14. LEGAL HOLD AND RETENTION REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-HOLD-001 - LEGAL HOLD OBJECT

A legal hold shall include:

- Hold ID;
- Issuing authority;
- Scope;
- Reason;
- Issued time;
- Issued by;
- Evidence IDs;
- Source IDs;
- Election/contest scope;
- Custodians;
- Systems covered;
- Review date;
- Release time; and
- Released by.

REQ-HOLD-002 - HOLD EFFECT

An active legal hold shall prevent:

- Evidence-object expiration;
- Artifact deletion;
- Log expiration;
- Source-configuration deletion;
- Release-image deletion;
- SBOM deletion;
- Key or certificate destruction that would impair verification;
- Timestamp-validation-material deletion; and
- Custody-record deletion.

REQ-HOLD-003 - HOLD AUDITABILITY

Placement, modification, review, and release of a legal hold shall require
authorization and shall create protected audit events.

REQ-HOLD-004 - RETENTION POLICY

A versioned retention policy shall define retention by evidence class,
election type, legal status, licensing restriction, and hold status. Automatic
disposition shall be disabled until legal and records-management review is
complete.

15. SEPARATION OF DUTIES AND PRIVILEGED-THREAT REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

REQ-SEC-001 - INDEPENDENT TRUST BOUNDARIES

The design shall separate, where practicable:

- Collection workers;
- Primary immutable archive;
- Secondary archive;
- Timestamp authorities;
- Manifest-signing keys;
- Audit-log store;
- Public tracker;
- Administrative control plane; and
- Verification environment.

REQ-SEC-002 - NO SINGLE-ADMINISTRATOR REWRITE

No single administrator or credential shall be capable of simultaneously:

- Modifying collector configuration;
- Replacing evidence in all archives;
- replacing manifest-signing keys;
- Altering audit logs;
- Removing external anchors; and
- Deleting retained evidence.

REQ-SEC-003 - DUAL CONTROL

The following actions shall require dual authorization or equivalent
segregation of duties:

- Retention-policy reduction;
- Legal-hold release;
- Evidence deletion after retention expiration;
- Signing-key replacement;
- Trust-anchor replacement;
- P0 source endpoint change during an active election;
- Audit-log policy change; and
- Disabling cross-boundary replication.

REQ-SEC-004 - BREAK-GLASS ACCESS

Emergency access shall be time limited, strongly authenticated, separately
logged, automatically reviewed, and incapable of suppressing its own audit
trail.

18. NEW ACCEPTANCE TESTS
======================================================================

AT-002-001 - ENDPOINT IDENTITY
Capture a controlled HTTPS source and verify preservation of DNS answers,
remote IP, TLS protocol, cipher suite, certificate, certificate chain,
fingerprint, SNI, and redirect chain.

AT-002-002 - CERTIFICATE CHANGE
Replace the test endpoint certificate and verify creation of an anomaly event
without loss of acquisition.

AT-002-003 - COMPLETE HTTP TRANSACTION
Verify that request, redirect, response, headers, body, timing, encoding, and
cache metadata are represented in the bundle manifest.

AT-002-004 - SENSITIVE HEADER CONTROL
Verify that secrets are absent from PUBLIC and RESEARCH packages, retained in
an encrypted restricted tier when required, and represented by a redaction or
commitment record.

AT-002-005 - DYNAMIC PAGE CAPTURE
Capture a page whose results load through an API call and verify preservation
of HTML, API response, DOM, screenshot, network log, browser metadata, and
component digests.

AT-002-006 - WEBSOCKET OR SSE UPDATE
Using a controlled source, verify capture or explicit limitation reporting for
results delivered through WebSocket or Server-Sent Events.

AT-002-007 - AUDIT LOG TAMPER DETECTION
Modify a copied audit-log segment and verify that signature or chain
validation fails.

AT-002-008 - CONFIGURATION CHANGE CONTROL
Change a P0 endpoint and verify author, reviewer, approval, test evidence,
activation time, configuration digest, and rollback data.

AT-002-009 - REPRODUCIBLE PARSER
Run the preserved parser container against a preserved artifact and verify the
stored parsed output or explain a deterministic, documented difference.

AT-002-010 - SBOM AND IMAGE VERIFICATION
Verify signed release, OCI image digest, SBOM, dependency lockfile, and build
provenance for a production collector.

AT-002-011 - ALGORITHM AGILITY
Add a second approved digest algorithm without schema migration or loss of the
prior digest.

AT-002-012 - EVIDENCE RECORD RENEWAL
Create a test evidence record, add a later archive timestamp/renewal, and
verify the complete timestamp chain without changing the original artifact.

AT-002-013 - DUAL ANCHOR
Verify an election-root manifest against two independent trust anchors.

AT-002-014 - CONTENT DEDUPLICATION
Acquire identical content repeatedly and verify one content artifact, multiple
acquisition events, and complete time/custody records.

AT-002-015 - MISSING DATA
Verify that zero votes, not published, source unavailable, parser failed, and
unknown are stored and displayed distinctly.

AT-002-016 - REVISION TAXONOMY
Replay normal count updates, source correction, parser correction, recount
adjustment, and amended certification; verify distinct classifications and
provenance.

AT-002-017 - PRESIDENTIAL DISTRICT ALLOCATION
Verify correct electoral-vote representation for statewide and congressional-
district allocations in Maine and Nebraska test datasets.

AT-002-018 - SENATE REGULAR AND SPECIAL
Verify simultaneous regular and special Senate contests remain distinct.

AT-002-019 - NON-VOTING HOUSE SEAT
Verify a delegate or resident-commissioner race does not alter the 218-seat
majority calculation.

AT-002-020 - CANDIDATE IDENTITY
Verify same-name candidates, write-ins, and multiple ballot lines are not
silently merged.

AT-002-021 - LEGAL HOLD
Apply a legal hold and verify prevention of artifact, log, image, certificate,
key-validation material, and configuration deletion.

AT-002-022 - SINGLE-ADMINISTRATOR LIMIT
Demonstrate that one administrator cannot rewrite all evidence copies, audit
logs, signatures, and external anchors.

AT-002-023 - EVIDENCE ACCESS PROFILE
Generate PUBLIC, RESEARCH, INTERNAL, LEGAL, and SECURITY packages and verify
field-level restrictions and redaction manifests.

AT-002-024 - BREAK-GLASS REVIEW
Exercise emergency access and verify strong authentication, time limit,
independent audit trail, and mandatory review.

======================================================================
22. CONCLUSION
======================================================================

Addendum 002 changes the evidentiary architecture from a raw-file archive with
strong timestamps into a complete acquisition-event system. The protected
record now includes the identity of the source endpoint, the HTTP or browser
transaction, the exact content, clock state, software environment, source
configuration, cryptographic proof, long-term evidence record, custody,
processing lineage, and external trust anchors.

It also closes national election-model gaps by defining election stages,
presidential allocation rules, Senate and House edge cases, candidate and
candidacy identity, missing-data states, and correction classifications.

The system shall preserve a narrow, verifiable factual claim: particular bytes
were acquired through a documented process, from a recorded endpoint, under a
recorded system configuration, and were cryptographically preserved and
independently anchored no later than the recorded attestation time. Broader
claims require the underlying evidence, context, and appropriate legal or
factual analysis.
